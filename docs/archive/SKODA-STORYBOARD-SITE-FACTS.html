What it is

Škoda Storyboard is Škoda Auto's global newsroom and press portal — a large editorial publication combining brand stories, official press releases, downloadable press kits, an automotive encyclopedia ("Škodapedia"), and a podcast. It is a content and media site, not an online shop: there is no product catalogue, pricing, or checkout. Its purpose is publishing news and providing journalists with media assets.

Key features & systems it runs

🔎

Search & filtered listings

Full site search plus content listings filterable across 14 topic categories (models, motorsport, technology, etc.), with "load more" paging.

🗂️

Media cart

Journalists can collect press images and assets and download them in bulk — a shopping-cart-style tool for media, not products.

📣

Promotional banner system

A built-in, per-market advertising system where ~46 banners are managed separately for each language market and shown to visitors based on the topic of the page they are reading.

✉️

Newsletter & subscriptions

A full subscriber system (sign-up, log-in, manage subscription) with consent handling — not just a simple sign-up box.

📚

Škodapedia encyclopedia

A self-contained, interactive A–Z glossary of ~189 (EN) automotive terms with a pop-up detail view — it runs on its own, needing no special live service behind it.

🎬

Rich media embeds

Video and audio embedded from Vimeo, YouTube, Buzzsprout (podcast), and Spotify.

📊

Visitor analytics

A built-in measurement system that tracks how visitors interact across the site — downloads, video plays, form sign-ups, banner clicks and "load more" — and feeds the company's reporting.

The media library

~28,300 distinct media items (roughly 200,000+ individual stored files) — the largest single part of the site. Predominantly photography (JPEG), alongside downloadable PDF press kits (some 25+ MB), high-resolution images, and video files for press use.

Most images sit in the galleries attached to press releases. Images carry descriptions and captions; a small number lack descriptive text.

Media types present

JPEG photography PDF press kits Hi-res downloads MP4 video Vimeo / YouTube Podcast audio SVG logos

Images are delivered in traditional formats (no next-generation formats such as WebP are in use today).

Beyond the pilot (full site)

Full production — all templates, the 4 back-end systems, ~28,300 media items (~200,000+ stored files), and all 6 languages — remains a multi-month programme (roughly 3–6 months with a small team). The automated import handles content and media at scale efficiently, but the back-end services, DAM setup, content rights, and translation volume are the schedule drivers — and AI helps least there.

Where AI helps most / least

  • Automated: content import, nav/footer generation, media pass-through, quality/visual checks
  • Assisted: page components & styling, listings/search front-end, test/fix loops
  • Least: the 4 back-end systems, DAM setup, content rights, translations

Estimates assume experienced engineers steering the AI and reviewing its output. "Days" = engineer-days. Actuals depend on the scope decisions still open (e.g. how many languages, and whether the 4 complex systems are rebuilt or set aside for the pilot).

In one glance

Škoda Storyboard is a large, mature, actively-published corporate newsroom. It combines ~13,300 editorial pages and ~28,300 media items across six languages on a customized WordPress platform, with sophisticated built-in systems for search, media distribution to journalists, targeted promotions, and newsletter subscriptions. In short: a high-volume publishing operation with a substantial press-media library at its core.
