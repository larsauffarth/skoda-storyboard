## Email 1 — Feasibility clarifications (re: walkthrough chapter 5, left part / media download)

Confirms which pieces need a small companion service vs. what EDS handles natively. **Nothing here is judged impossible within AEM.**

- **Zipping an arbitrary cart selection** — a static site can't package a journalist's ad-hoc pick into one ZIP on the fly. Needs a **small service (App Builder + AEM Assets)** to do size-reduction + packaging. **Demo fallback: selected files download separately.** (Aligns with the 14 Sep proposal — see [`20260914.md`](20260914.md) §9.) A **whole press-kit ZIP is different** — it's a pre-built file, so that one is easy.
- **Search & filtering at full library scale** — the content index handles listings and index-based search well for a **representative set**; true search + faceting across **tens of thousands of assets** with ranking wants a **hosted search service**. The demo proves the experience on a sample.
- **Transcoding non-image originals** — the platform resizes/reformats **images** natively (already serves smaller web renditions on the fly), but does **not** process **PDFs or video**; those are referenced from the DAM **as-is**.
- **Gated end-user access (the QR journey)** — no built-in per-visitor gating; needs a **defined access model and likely a small service**. To be designed together.
- **Overall:** no part is deemed impossible to implement within AEM.
