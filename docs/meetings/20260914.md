## 2. Site Structure — Two Connected Properties

Storyboard and Media Room are **two connected parts of the same website** with different use cases:

- **Storyboard** — stories/articles on various topics for users seeking insights. Primary navigation: **Models** (global models only — no regional/country-specific models), **E-mobility**, **Lifestyle** (subsections: IT/tech, People, Sports, Adventure), **Škoda World**, **Series**, **Škodapedia** ("Škoda PDN"/A–Z list). Plus dropdown options.
- **Media Room** — exclusively supports media/press around launches, with assets and downloads. Authored primarily by the **headquarters team** for events and media use cases.

**Unification intent:** header, primary navigation, footer, search, and language selector should be **unified across both properties** so the experience is consistent. Today the two footers differ; unification may be out of PoC scope but is a stated future direction.

## 4. Storyboard — Homepage Composition (top → bottom)

1. **Featured / hero articles** — one large landscape image + 2 additional cards. Supports **both** author-selected featured articles **and** default = latest. (Resolved an Adobe assumption: not purely auto-populated — authors can select, default falls back to latest.)
2. **Latest Stories** — auto-populated feed; may also surface Media Room content. Default layout: **two rows** — a 50/50 two-card row followed by a three-card row. **"Load more"** appends in the same 2+3 pattern; extendable.
3. **Social media** — links to Škoda Global channels; click launches the platform (YouTube etc.). *Traffic is primarily mobile — called out explicitly.*
4. **Models** — displays ~4 full + a 5th peeking/overflow card with forward navigation. **Exception to the card pattern:** uses a model image (not an article hero) instead of the main article picture + headline.
5. **E-mobility** — stories on battery cars etc.; default 3-across + overflow (3.5) with a "select all / show all" option.
6. **Škoda World** — articles from that section.
7. **Series** — a distinct strip rendered on an **author-selected background colour**; open question on exactly how Series should be displayed/modelled.
8. **Latest News** — rolled in **from Media Room** (latest published press/article news), ordered by publish date.

## 5. Storyboard — Article Template

Structure of a single story:
- Full-bleed hero image, headline, **published date**, and **category** (the story's **primary** category; additional categories can also be tagged and displayed → an article can belong to **more than one category**).
- Rich body text in multiple styles/forms, interleaved with standalone images and **carousels/sliders**.
- **Carousel behaviour is mixed:** some carousels **autoplay** on page load; others are **manual navigation only**. (Flagged for the build: likely needs distinct carousel variants — currently modelled as one common component in the requirements doc.)

### 5.1 Media Box (Storyboard variant)
- Holds assets related to the story (images; videos if present).
- Per-asset: **add to cart**, choosing **original size or a specific pixel size**; then download from the cart. Individual assets can also be downloaded directly (original or specific size).
- Top-right control to **add/select all** assets of the story in one action.
- Default shows **4 assets** with a **"show more"**.
- **Editorial behaviour (Markus):** authors currently add all article-related assets **manually**. It would be acceptable for the system to auto-populate all assets, **provided authors can delete individual images** from the media box (e.g. exclude a diagram/PDF). Media box normally contains the images related to the article, with small exceptions.

### 5.2 Related / Recommended content (two mechanisms)
- **"Explore more" / recommendation box (right-hand + top):** up to **3 articles selectable by hand**; if not manually set, falls back to articles sharing the **same tag**. Manual override is important — authors can influence it.
- **Related-stories strip (bottom):** pulled from the **tags/categories** on the article (e.g. tags like `2026`, `epic`).

### 5.3 Newsletter box
- Appears on the article page. **PoC = UI only** (see §3).

## 6. Language Model (critical for site setup)

- **Per-article language availability is dynamic:** an article/story shows only the languages it is actually authored in. Homepage/landing may show ~4; a story view may show 6, etc. The language switcher reflects **only available languages** for that content.
- **Country adaptation is optional, not mandatory:** HQ typically authors in **English + Czech**; countries **choose** whether to adapt an article into their language. If a country does not adapt it, **it does not appear in that country's navigation.**
- **Language versions are NOT 100% identical:** ~80–90% overlap; the remaining ~10–20% is country-specific. Countries can author **their own** articles (e.g. a German-only topic) that appear only on their side. HQ may later copy such an article into English if desired.
- **Translation workflow is manual (WordPress today):** on an article, a right-hand menu lets the author **select a new language**, which creates a **copy carrying the source structure/text**; the author then **pastes in the translated text** and adjusts for market. This copy-to-new-language flow is used often and is an **important back-end/editorial feature** to preserve. (Could not be shown live due to WordPress access limits.)

## 8. Media Room — Structure & Behaviours

Primary navigation: **News** (press releases), **Press Kits**, **Models**, **Images**, **Videos**, **Companies/Corporate**, **Škoda Media**. Default language coverage is narrower (**EN / CZ / SK** — HQ languages), narrowing further to the languages actually available once you reach a specific press release/article.

### 8.1 Advanced filter (common across Media Room listings)
- Two levels: a primary filter set (defaults to "all" selections across Media Room) + a secondary set (filter by **year / event / history**).
- Same filter pattern appears across News, Images, Videos, etc.
- **Open question:** should the filter set be static ("all") or adapt dynamically to the selected section?

### 8.2 Listings & pagination
- Press-release listing shows a **total count** (~1,651 press releases from 2015) and **6-card display** with pagination; **"load more" adds 6** each time. Same pagination logic reused across Images, Videos, etc.
- **Sort:** newest / oldest.

### 8.3 Press release / article (Media Room) — differences vs Storyboard article
- Adds a **generated audio** section (third-party TTS, language-matched to the page) — see §3 audio decision.
- **Videos:** external (mostly **Vimeo**, also **YouTube**); support play, add-to-basket/cart download, and **copy link**. Physically stored in Storyboard, can be added to cart.
- **Media Box (Media Room variant):** richer than Storyboard — includes **images, videos, PDFs, and infographics**. Assets can be added to cart individually or the whole box selected at once.

### 8.4 Press Kits (most complex area)
- A press kit (e.g. "Škoda Epic Plus") is broken into **sections** (Introduction, Exterior, Interior, Battery/Safety, Connectivity, …).
- Each section can expose **Text (downloadable), Infographics, Technical Data, Images, Videos**.
- **Content availability is not uniform** — a given topic/section may lack technical data, infographics, etc. **The UI shifts based on what exists** (empty categories are omitted). ⇒ **QA action:** verify across content how the UI behaves when a category is absent (does it disappear cleanly?). Confirmed behaviour is inconsistent across topics.
- **Download kit** = server-side **ZIP packaging** of selected assets, segregated by asset type.

### 8.5 Images & Videos (Media Room)
- **Consolidated asset galleries** spanning **both** Storyboard and Media Room (all images used in Storyboard also appear here). Filterable by topic. Same load-more/pagination.

## 9. Media Download / Cart — Feasibility Question (Adobe)

- Current WordPress behaviour: selected assets are **packed into a ZIP** by a back-end service; the cart/basket also offers a whole-package ZIP download, segregated by asset type.
- **ZIP packaging is a back-end service outside the EDS sphere** — an external dependency Adobe must confirm can be built in time.
- **Adobe proposal for the demo:** build the **selection UI** and, on one click, **download the selected assets individually** (not pre-packed into a ZIP) if the packaging service can't be ready. **Client (Jaroslav) receptive** ("why not") but wants it discussed/agreed with the business owner (Eda Horák) rather than unilaterally decided.

## 12. Editorial / Back-End Requirements Called Out

- **Confidential / pre-publication content (Confluence §6.5):** protected content for upcoming car launches, accessible to a **specific group** of authors (group-level, not per-individual), invisible to others. Today handled via a **separate WordPress folder** with group access.
  - **Adobe proposal (Lars):** use native EDS access control — guard a folder/area by group access rights, and treat pre-publication as a **preview that is not yet live**, published at a chosen time. Client (Markus) fine with sharing a whole article as a restricted preview to a few people; a separate folder is not required if a simpler EDS mechanism exists. **Group-level security layer required.**
- **Author history / audit log:** client requirement to keep a **historical log of all authoring changes by user**. Important because **multiple agencies** author content (one agency for Media Room, one for Storyboard, plus sub-agencies, plus country agencies) — need to trace who changed what on a public-facing site. Possibly an EDS/AEM out-of-the-box capability to confirm.
